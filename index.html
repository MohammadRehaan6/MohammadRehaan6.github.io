About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at SRK Institute of Technology, Vijayawada, specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. Most of what I build sits at the intersection of practical software engineering and applied AI — chat interfaces, recommendation systems, and sentiment analysis tools that solve a specific, real problem rather than showcase a model in isolation.

Outside coursework, I'm drawn to civic technology — using software for public-interest problems — and I've contributed to a project tied to the Viksit Bharat initiative. I compete regularly in hackathons, which is where most of my projects started as weekend prototypes before becoming something more complete.
